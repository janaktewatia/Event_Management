# Finding SMTP Settings in Zoho Mail

## 🔍 Different Zoho Interfaces

Zoho has different layouts. Let's find your settings!

---

## Method 1: Modern Zoho Interface (Most Common)

1. Go to https://mail.zoho.com
2. Click **Settings** (⚙️ gear icon, top right)
3. Look for **Preferences** in the left sidebar
4. Click **Mail Preferences**
5. Scroll down to find **IMAP/SMTP** or **Security**

---

## Method 2: Classic Zoho Interface

1. Go to https://mail.zoho.com
2. Click **Settings** (⚙️ gear icon, top right)
3. Click **General** tab
4. Scroll to find **IMAP/SMTP** section

---

## Method 3: Zoho One/Admin Console

If you have Zoho One:

1. Go to https://accounts.zoho.com
2. Click **Settings**
3. Click **Mail** in left menu
4. Look for **Protocol Access** or **IMAP/SMTP**

---

## Method 4: Direct Link (Try This!)

Try going directly to:
```
https://mail.zoho.com/settings?tab=general
```

This might take you straight to settings.

---

## 🤔 What If You Can't Find IMAP/SMTP?

**This might mean:**
- IMAP/SMTP is already enabled (good!)
- Your Zoho account type doesn't require explicit enabling
- You can proceed with SMTP credentials directly

---

## ✅ Alternative: Just Try the SMTP Directly

Since you can't find the IMAP/SMTP setting, **it might already be enabled**.

Try these credentials in Communication Setup:

```
Provider: Zoho Mail
Email: your.email@zohomail.com
Password: [Your Zoho login password]
SMTP Host: smtp.zoho.com
SMTP Port: 587
```

Click **Test Connection** and see if it works!

---

## 🌍 If You're in Different Region

If it doesn't work with `smtp.zoho.com`, try your region:

| Region | SMTP Host |
|--------|-----------|
| US | `smtp.zoho.com` |
| Europe | `smtp.zoho.eu` |
| India | `smtp.zoho.in` |
| Australia | `smtp.zoho.com.au` |
| China | `smtp.zoho.com.cn` |

Check your Zoho URL to find your region.
